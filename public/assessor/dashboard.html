Stage 1 — Submission & completeness check

Gate 0 · not scored

A coordinator checks the submission can be assessed. Mark each item Yes (present) or No (missing). Nothing is judged on quality yet — an incomplete submission is returned to the provider, not failed, and assessment stays locked until every item is Yes.

Coordinator notes

Stage 2 — Desk review: evidence matrix scoring

Score every document-evidenced indicator with evidence cited — “looks fine” is not an assessment. Mandatory gates are Met / Not met only. Anything inapplicable is N/A automatically from the case setup.

Stage 3 — Live verification

Delivery indicators verified in the wild: observation, mystery-learner pass, and support routes tested for real. Notes should be timestamped and location-anchored.

Observation record

Stage 4 — Independent derivations

1.4.3 derived · 4.1 sampled

Re-derive the CPD hours from the materials. The tolerance check applies automatically: a claim more than 20% above your derivation fails gate 1.4.3. Share the arithmetic with the provider — a disputed number is an argument about arithmetic, not opinion.

Stage 5 — Moderation (second assessor)

Reliability gate · no provider contact

A second assessor with no provider contact reviews the full matrix before any decision exists. Approve each check, or push it back with a reason for the lead assessor to address. The decision is arithmetic plus gates — not mood.

Sign in — your initials are stamped on each approval.

Stage 6 — Decision & remediation loop

One deferral · 8 weeks · then final

The fix list below is generated live from the failing mandatory gates, grouped into jobs by the cause you set on each notice — one job, one notice, however many gates it clears. After the provider resubmits, re-score the affected indicators in Stages 2–4 (plus a regression sample) — the decision recomputes automatically. There is no second deferral.

Stage 8 — Surveillance & renewal

C4 · annual cycle · 3-year term

Accreditation is a cycle with monitoring, not a one-off purchase. Two annual surveillance reviews fall due on the anniversary of accreditation: the provider submits its year’s evidence, and you sample for drift — especially whether the 1.2 review cycle has actually been run and whether marketing claims stay honest (6.3, A5). The due dates below are computed from the accreditation date; anything due or overdue is flagged on every screen and on the caseload dashboard.
